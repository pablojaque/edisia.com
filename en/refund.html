Since we work on custom projects rather than selling standard digital products, our refund policy is based on actual project progress.

1. Before the project starts

If you paid a deposit and decide to cancel before we start working, we refund 100% of the amount paid.

2. Project in progress

If the project is already underway, we refund the portion corresponding to work not yet done, deducting the hours and resources already spent up to the point of cancellation.

3. Delivered project

Once the work has been delivered and approved, no refunds are issued, unless there's a demonstrable breach of what was agreed in writing.
