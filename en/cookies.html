Cookie Policy

Last updated: September 6, 2026

1. What cookies are

Cookies are small files a website stores in your browser to remember information, such as your preferences.

2. What cookies we use

This site only uses browser local storage to remember your choice about this same cookie notice (accept or reject). We don't use analytics, advertising, or tracking cookies of any kind, and there are no third-party scripts installed on the site.

3. How to manage cookies

You can choose "Accept" or "Reject" in the notice that appears when you enter the site. You can also clear the data stored by the site at any time from your browser settings.

4. Changes to this policy

If we add any additional cookie in the future (for example, for analytics), we will update this page and ask for your consent before activating it.
