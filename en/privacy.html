Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 6, 2026

At Barmaja we take the privacy of visitors and people who contact us seriously. This policy explains what data we collect, what we use it for, and what rights you have over it.

1. Data controller

Barmaja is responsible for the data collected through this site. Contact: pablojaquevfx@gmail.com.

2. What data we collect

We only collect the data you voluntarily give us through the contact form: name, email address, business (if you provide it), and your message. We don't ask for data we don't need to reply to you.

3. What we use your data for

We use this data solely to answer your inquiry and, if we move forward with a project, to coordinate it. We don't use your data for advertising or sell it to third parties.

4. Legal basis

We process your data based on your explicit consent, given by checking the acceptance box on the contact form before submitting it.

5. Cookies

This site does not use cookies. We only store your light/dark mode preference in your browser's local storage, which is never shared or used for tracking or advertising.

6. Who we share your data with

We don't share your personal data with third parties for commercial purposes. We use Vercel Analytics to see, in aggregate and anonymized form, how many people visit the site and which pages they view; it doesn't use cookies or identify individual visitors. You can read Vercel's privacy policy at vercel.com/legal/privacy-policy.

7. How long we keep your data

We keep contact data only for as long as necessary to handle your inquiry or for the duration of the business relationship, and delete it afterward unless we're legally required to keep it.

8. Your rights

You can ask us at any time to access, correct, or delete your data, or object to its processing, by writing to pablojaquevfx@gmail.com.

9. Security

We apply reasonable measures to protect your data against unauthorized access, loss, or misuse.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy occasionally. Any changes will be posted on this same page along with the updated date.

11. Contact

For any questions about this policy, write to pablojaquevfx@gmail.com.
